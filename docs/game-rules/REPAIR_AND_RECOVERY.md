# Repair, Recovery and Ship-Loss Rules

**Status:** NOT_STARTED — no game-rule assumptions are authorized from this file  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-06

## Research scope

- Dock assignment, repair duration, repair completion, and repair-bucket behavior.
- Ship HP/damage-state transitions and recovery.
- Retreat and escort-retreat behavior, including Combined Fleet restrictions.
- Sinking/loss conditions and any irreversible ship-loss boundary.
- Fatigue interactions with repair and recovery.
- Engagement-type expedition damage and recovery.

## Required evidence

Prioritize official rules and safe, passive observation. Never deliberately test a ship-loss boundary or risk a valuable ship merely to fill a documentation gap.

## Prohibited assumptions

- Do not infer that a ship is safe to advance from a single HP snapshot.
- Do not equate "damaged" and "heavily damaged" without a verified threshold.
- Do not implement automated retreat or continue behavior until the exact game decision rules and failure consequences are documented.
